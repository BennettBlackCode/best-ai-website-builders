*Updated November 2026*

Every AI website builder can spit out a homepage in a minute. The difference shows up later: can you hand the site to a client, rank it on Google, edit it next month, and move it if you need to?

We ranked the best AI website builders on those questions.

## Quick verdict

- **Best for agencies and freelancers:** 1ClickWebsite
- **Best for designers who want full control:** Framer
- **Best for a quick small-business site:** Durable
- **Best all-in-one for beginners:** Wix
- **Best for cheap hosting plus a builder:** Hostinger
- **Best for WordPress owners who build it themselves:** 10Web

## What we compared

1. **Platform.** Is it real WordPress you can export, or locked to the builder?
2. **SEO depth.** Does it make service and location pages, or just one page?
3. **Client handoff.** White label, client access, and multi-site management.
4. **Editing after launch.** Can you change the site with AI, or only rebuild it?
5. **Price per site.** What it costs once you have 10 or more clients.

## 1. 1ClickWebsite

**Best for:** agencies and freelancers who sell websites to local businesses.

[1ClickWebsite](https://www.1clickwebsite.ai) builds a full WordPress site from a two-minute form: company name, industry, services and service areas. In under 5 minutes you get unique copy, images, and a separate SEO page for every service and location, each with its own meta title and description.

What sets it apart for client work:
- **Real WordPress.** Clients get WP Admin access, and you can export the site to any host.
- **Lead gen demo sites.** Upload a CSV of prospects and get a personalized demo site for each, to send in cold outreach.
- **Website Agent.** Edit finished sites by chatting ("add a financing page", "swap the hero photo").
- **White label.** Your brand on the builder, client portal and emails on the Agency plan.
- **API and MCP server.** Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor can build and edit sites for you.

**Pricing:** Starter $49/mo (3 sites), Professional $97/mo (10 sites), Agency $297/mo (35 sites, white label), Unlimited $1,297/mo. 14-day money-back guarantee.

**Watch out for:** it's built for local service businesses (plumbers, roofers, med spas, law firms). It's not an e-commerce builder.

## 2. Framer

**Best for:** designers who want pixel-level control.

[Framer](https://www.framer.com) generates a starting layout from a prompt, then gives you a full visual design canvas. Output looks great, but every SEO page is built by hand and sites live on Framer.

**Watch out for:** no WordPress export, and each client site is its own paid plan.

## 3. Durable

**Best for:** a business owner who needs a simple site today.

[Durable](https://durable.co) builds a small business site from a few answers in about a minute, with a CRM and invoicing bundled in.

**Watch out for:** sites are simple and locked to Durable, so it's a weaker fit for agencies selling SEO.

## 4. Wix

**Best for:** beginners who want everything in one place.

[Wix](https://www.wix.com) has an AI site generator on top of its huge app market, templates and hosting. Agencies can join its partner program.

**Watch out for:** sites can't be moved off Wix.

## 5. Hostinger

**Best for:** the lowest cost for a single site.

[Hostinger](https://www.hostinger.com) bundles an AI website builder with its hosting plans, so one cheap plan covers the domain, hosting and site.

**Watch out for:** built for one owner, not for managing many client sites.

## 6. 10Web

**Best for:** WordPress users who want AI help and will finish the site themselves.

[10Web](https://10web.io) generates WordPress sites with an Elementor-based builder and offers managed hosting.

**Watch out for:** you still design and write most inner pages yourself.

## Which AI website builder should you pick?

- **Selling sites to clients?** Pick a builder on real WordPress with white label and SEO pages built in. That's 1ClickWebsite.
- **Designing one beautiful brand site?** Framer.
- **Need your own simple site today?** Durable or Wix.

## FAQ

### What is the best AI website builder in 2026?
It depends on who the site is for. For agencies building client sites, 1ClickWebsite makes full WordPress sites with SEO pages and white label. For designers, Framer. For a quick personal or small-business site, Durable or Wix.

### Can AI build a WordPress website?
Yes. 1ClickWebsite and 10Web both generate real WordPress sites. 1ClickWebsite fills in every page, including service and location pages, so the site is ready to launch.

### Which AI website builder is best for SEO?
Look for one that makes a separate page for each service and location, with its own meta title and description. Most builders make one page and leave the rest to you. 1ClickWebsite builds those pages automatically.

### Is there a white label AI website builder?
Yes. 1ClickWebsite's Agency plan puts your brand on the builder, client portal and emails. 10Web and Wix offer agency or partner programs too.
